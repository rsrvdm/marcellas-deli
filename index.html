Welcome to Marcella's

We're a family-style deli on Wharf Street, Forster, making food the way it should be: fresh, simple and full of flavour.

Our panini are served on house-baked ciabatta rolls, our focaccia (including gluten-free) comes out of the oven fresh, and our shelves are stocked with quality Italian pasta, cured meats and cheeses.

Pop in for a coffee and a bite, or build a picnic to enjoy by the water.
